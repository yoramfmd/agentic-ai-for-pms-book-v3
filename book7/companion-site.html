The companion site

Ostermill Industrial Supply does not exist. Chapter 2 says so, and says what the fiction is allowed to carry and what it is not.

The site exists. It holds the fuller versions of the documents this book reproduces as extracts, the three prototypes as pages you can read rather than a table you have to take on trust, and the project record in the order it was actually made.
