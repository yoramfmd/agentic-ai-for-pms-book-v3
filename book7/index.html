Agentic AI for Product Leaders

One agent, one company, from the sentence that proposed it to the year after it launched

By Yoram Friedman

The first four books are built out of documented incidents and cited research. This one is built the other way, around a single company that does not exist: Ostermill Industrial Supply, followed from the sentence that proposed an agent to the year after it launched.

Part One is the argument in ten chapters. Part Two is the case in five phases, carrying the thirty procedure cards and eleven case-file exhibits the project produced. Where it compresses an argument, the other four are where that argument is made at length and where it can be checked.
