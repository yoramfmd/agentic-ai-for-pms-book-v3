About the author

Practice, before theory.

Yoram Friedman, MD, is a physician and enterprise product leader. He has shipped enterprise software across SAP, Walmart, Microsoft, and regulated health technology, working on semantic data architecture, AI-ready data products, and systems that operate under real regulatory constraint. He trained in anesthesiology and radiology, and writes on agentic AI, clinical workflow, and the product discipline that emerges when software starts making decisions on its own.

The regulatory and design default for AI is “keep a human in the loop.” Sustained AI use erodes the human’s ability to perform that loop’s function. This series is for the people who have to design supervision that does not assume the supervisor is the unimpaired version of themselves they were on day one.
