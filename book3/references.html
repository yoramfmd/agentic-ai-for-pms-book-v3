Consolidated bibliographic references for the empirical claims in the book, organized by the chapter where each is primarily anchored. This page is the complete consolidated reference list. Articles by the author at data-decisions-and-clinics.com are cited inline rather than expanded here.

A note on sourcing. The evidence tiers used throughout this book are defined in the preface, in “A note on the evidence”: peer-reviewed studies and primary regulatory text; documented industry incidents; litigation-stage allegations (presented as alleged); vendor and company claims (labeled as such); the author’s own cases; and a few illustrative composites (flagged on the page where they appear). The list below is the working bibliography, grouped by the chapter where each source is primarily anchored, with the tier noted where it is not obvious from the citation itself. This list gives the short-form bibliography; the fuller primary-source treatment of each case is compiled separately. Dates and figures in a fast-moving field are stamped to mid-2026 and should be re-verified before publication.
